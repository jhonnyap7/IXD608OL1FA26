Paragraphs

Sophea creates spatial fragrances designed to shape the atmosphere of a room. Each scent is composed to bring a sense of clarity, warmth, and intention into everyday spaces.

Our collection explores fragrance through a quiet and architectural approach. From the brightness of CURRENT to the depth of OBSIDIAN and the warmth of DUNE, each candle creates a distinct mood while remaining part of one considered collection.

Thoughtful materials, restrained design, and carefully selected fragrance notes define the SOPHEA experience. Every detail is intentional, creating objects that feel as considered in a space as the fragrance they release.
